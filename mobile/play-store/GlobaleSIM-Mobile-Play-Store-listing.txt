FULL DESCRIPTION
----------------
GlobaleSIM Mobile is a simple US phone plan: your own phone number, calling minutes and a US data eSIM — all in one app.

ONE APP FOR YOUR NUMBER AND YOUR DATA
• Your own US phone number for calls to and from the US and Canada
• A US data eSIM that refills every month on the same eSIM
• See your data left and minutes left at a glance
• Running low? Add data in a few taps — no new eSIM, no new QR code

CALLS THAT SAVE YOUR DATA
• On Wi-Fi, calls use no mobile data
• Out and about, calls use only a little data over 4G/5G
• The app shows whether you're on Wi-Fi or cellular

EVERYTHING A PHONE NEEDS
• Keypad, recent calls and voicemail
• Incoming calls ring even when the app is closed
• Receive text messages
• 911 with your location (US)

PLANS
Choose Basic, Standard or Plus at mobile.globalesim.tel — pay monthly with PayPal and cancel any time. Then sign in to this app with the same email.

For personal (residential) use. Your phone must support eSIM.
